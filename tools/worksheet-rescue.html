Things to know before using the tool

Can this remove handwriting and reveal words underneath?

No. You can selectively lighten red or blue marks and manually paint over small areas. If handwriting covers printed words, the covered words are not reliably recoverable. Do not use this for restoring examination answers that must remain accurate.

Can I upload a PDF directly?

This first release accepts image files, not PDFs. If you have a scanned PDF, export its pages as images first. PDF input is a possible future feature.

Why do some fine pencil lines disappear?

Strong background cleanup or black-and-white sensitivity can remove faint marks. Lower contrast, decrease background removal or choose Keep original colors before exporting.

Are the files stored on your server?

No. Images are processed locally using your browser's canvas. They are not transmitted to our server by the editor. AdSense, if present, is a separate third-party integration.

Can I combine more than one worksheet?

Yes. Add up to 20 images, use the up/down controls to arrange them and download one printable PDF.
