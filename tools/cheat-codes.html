01 / GET STARTED

THREE THINGS TO KNOW.

01

FINISH THE STORY

Launch guides say cheat unlocks are gated by story completion, and many require individual challenges.

02

OPEN THE PHONE

Reported input: D-pad Up, hold L1 + press R1 (PlayStation) or LB + RB (Xbox), followed by the cheat's directional code.

03

PROTECT YOUR SAVE

Published guides warn that using cheats can disable saving, achievements or trophies for that session. Save before testing.
