TROUBLESHOOTING / BEFORE TRYING AGAIN

WHEN IT DOESN'T OPEN.

01

CHECK YOUR LOCATION

Similar-looking doors can belong to different objectives. A motel code will not open a boatworks safe.

02

GET THE CLUE

Wall maps, notes and staff dialogue are often more trustworthy than numbers copied from social media.

03

WATCH FOR RANDOMIZATION

Robbery and store cash safes can vary. We do not present one number as universal when it may be generated.
