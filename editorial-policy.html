Editorial policy

Research and sourcing

We prioritize relevant official sources on safety, airline regulations, customs and other changeable information. Our guides distinguish general planning suggestions from legal or official requirements.

Experience and images

We do not invent site visits, interviews, expertise or reviews. Editorial images are illustrative and should not be interpreted as depicting the precise location discussed.

Reviews and corrections

Publication and research dates are included on articles. Important information can change; readers should verify requirements before travelling. Corrections are welcomed through our published contact channels.
