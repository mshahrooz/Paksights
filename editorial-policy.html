EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How we investigate GTA VI claims, handle speculation and correct mistakes.

Source-first reporting

For launch facts, platform news, price changes, settings and availability, we prefer dated primary sources from Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, official platform listings and relevant rating boards. Each substantive article includes a source section linking directly to its evidence.

Confirmed vs reported vs speculation

Confirmed means directly supported by an authoritative source or plainly visible documented evidence. Reported means attributed to a named source, whose claim may not be independently validated. Speculation is plainly labeled and is never presented as official fact. We do not invent PC dates, online release schedules, map measurements or gameplay systems.

Spoiler-aware coverage

Launch coverage avoids major story spoilers unless a page gives a clear warning before revealing them. We do not use unmarked leaked plot material to bait search traffic.

Corrections and updates

Every article includes a publication date and site-wide source-check date. When a fact changes, we revise the article and cite the new source. Major factual corrections should be noted within the page. Report concerns via our contact page.

Ads and independence

Google AdSense can appear on the site. Ad placements are separate from articles. No advertiser can buy a favorable factual conclusion in our editorial coverage.

Media policy

We link to official Rockstar media and use limited official screenshots for editorial identification and analysis with rights-holder credit. If you own rights to any material and would like to discuss a use, contact the publisher. Read our media credits.
