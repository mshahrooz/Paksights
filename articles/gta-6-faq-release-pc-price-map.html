VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ Is GTA 6 out?

Yes. Rockstar announced worldwide availability on November 19, 2026.

02/ What platforms is it on?

The officially announced native launch systems are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

03/ Is GTA 6 on PC?

Rockstar has not announced a native PC edition or PC launch date in the official November 2026 documentation covered by this guide.

04/ How much does GTA 6 cost?

Take-Two’s announced US prices are $79.99 for Standard Edition and $99.99 for Ultimate Edition. Regional prices and taxes can differ.

05/ What is the GTA 6 map called?

The fictional state is Leonida. Named areas include Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

06/ Who are the protagonists?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the main pair identified in the promotional materials.

07/ What is the age rating?

The ESRB lists Grand Theft Auto VI as Mature 17+ and describes adult content including intense violence, strong language and sexual content. It is not a game intended for children.

08/ Does the physical version include a disc?

Rockstar Support states that the physical retail package contains a download code rather than a game disc. Check your region and storefront before purchase.
