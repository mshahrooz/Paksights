GTA VI JUST GOT 23 NEW SONGS.

A surprise Deluxe Edition. A 57-track soundtrack. Travis Scott, Slayyyter, The Dare and more—plus fresh official music videos from Atlantic Records.

Four days after the game arrived, Rockstar and Atlantic Records have made the musical world of Grand Theft Auto VI even bigger. On Monday, November 23, 2026, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition), adding 23 bonus tracks to an already massive 34-song album. That is 57 tracks in one official collection.

VERIFIED, NOT RUMORED: The news comes directly from Rockstar's November 23 announcement. The 57-song total and artists are corroborated by Pitchfork and NME. This is a music release, not new missions or an Online launch.

01 / Rockstar's surprise post-launch soundtrack drop

The original companion album, released around GTA VI's November 19 game launch, already had 34 new songs and names spanning pop, hip-hop, Latin and electronic music. Rockstar's November 20 music announcement highlighted Playboi Carti, Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, JENNIE, Don Toliver, JHAYCO, Zara Larsson, Gunna and Burna Boy.

Yet just days later, the record label and publisher unveiled another 23 songs. The timing gives fans a new reason to revisit the soundtrack outside their game sessions, and it puts the series' long-running radio culture back in the spotlight. The Deluxe Edition is now streaming, according to Rockstar.

For accuracy, one distinction matters: Rockstar describes these additions as tracks from GTA VI, but its short Deluxe announcement does not specify the exact in-game station for every song. We aren't inventing station assignments or suggesting the game itself received extra missions. The official music hub is the place to verify the full listing.

02 / The new artists and songs making noise

The surprise package is a cross-genre expansion rather than a single-artist drop. Rockstar specifically names The Dare, Slayyyter, Denzel Curry, HEALTH, Power Trip, and Real Dimez with Sexyy Red. Music coverage also highlights Travis Scott, 21 Savage and Overmono among the artists associated with the new tracks.

06 / Deluxe album versus the Justice original score: what's different?

Fans searching “GTA 6 soundtrack” may see two different official releases. The expanded 57-track Deluxe Edition is the vocal-artist companion album. The separate GTA VI: Original Score, composed by the French electronic duo Justice, is listed for December 11, 2026. Rockstar describes a 30-track original score collection with further digital bonus material.

They are separate releases. The December score date should not be confused with the already available November Deluxe album. For the latest exact details and complete credits, see Rockstar's music section.

07 / Your GTA VI music questions, answered

How many songs are on GTA 6: The Album Deluxe Edition?

There are 57 in total: 34 original-album tracks plus 23 bonuses added on November 23, 2026.

Is the Deluxe Edition soundtrack out now?

Yes. Rockstar announced it was available on major streaming platforms on November 23.

Does the new soundtrack mean GTA VI has new missions?

No. The announcement concerns a companion album, not an in-game mission update.

Are all 57 songs confirmed on specific in-game radio stations?

No station-by-station placement is established for all 57 tracks in the brief official announcement. Consult Rockstar's music directory.

When is the Justice GTA VI Original Score released?

Rockstar lists December 11, 2026 for the separate official score collection.
