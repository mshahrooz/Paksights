When Is GTA 6 Online Coming Out? Release Date, Multiplayer & 2027 Rumors

GTA 6 Online is not available at launch. Get the latest November 24 release-date status, the 2027 rumor, crossplay and character transfer facts, and official update links.

Five days after GTA VI's console debut, players are asking when they can explore Leonida together. Here is the current answer, the evidence behind it, and what the latest 2027 speculation really means.
Release status: NOT CONFIRMED

Last checked November 24, 2026. The campaign is out, but an official GTA VI multiplayer launch date has not been published.

01/ Is GTA 6 Online out now?

No. As of November 24, 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as a story-driven single-player game. Rockstar has not announced a release date for a GTA VI multiplayer mode. The company's June 24 preorder announcement explicitly described the game as a single-player experience; its November 19 launch announcement confirms the console release.

That means there is no official GTA 6 Online lobby to unlock by completing a mission, changing a setting or paying for an alleged early-access code. Internet connectivity for installation, account services and updates is a separate matter from multiplayer.

02/ Could GTA 6 Online release in 2027?

The question has become particularly topical this morning. The Independent's November 24 report notes that Twitch CEO Dan Clancy has suggested a possible 2027 arrival. The Sun's November 24 coverage also highlights the absence of a Rockstar release date. Neither is an official launch announcement.

A suggestion from an executive at a streaming platform may reflect expectations about a major entertainment event, but it does not establish a publisher's timetable. No month, day, beta date or launch time should be treated as settled on this basis. Even a plausible window can move as development and testing progress.

Our editorial assessment is that waiting for Rockstar's own announcement is more useful than counting down to an invented date. This page will distinguish new primary-source information from commentary whenever the situation changes.

03/ What GTA 5's launch teaches us

Rockstar has separated a Grand Theft Auto story launch from its multiplayer release before. GTA V reached stores on September 17, 2013, while the first GTA Online launched on October 1. That two-week gap was documented by Rockstar in advance, including an August 2013 gameplay announcement and its GTA V release-date announcement.

The important difference is that Rockstar publicly scheduled GTA V's online component before that game arrived. Applying the same 14-day delay to GTA VI would produce a speculative calendar date, not a fact. A separate launch could allow attention to remain on the campaign and provide more preparation time for network services, but Rockstar has not confirmed its reasons or plans.

04/ Crossplay, character transfers, heists and roleplay

Players want to know whether friends on PlayStation and Xbox can meet in the same session, whether existing GTA Online characters and money carry over, and whether there will be cooperative heists or custom roleplay servers. All are reasonable questions; none should be answered as confirmed GTA VI functionality without an official feature announcement.

If your group is considering buying different console versions, avoid assuming crossplay will solve the platform split. Likewise, do not spend money on GTA V's online economy with the expectation that progress will automatically move to a future title.

05/ Why multiplayer interest is growing

There is a substantial audience already exploring Leonida. Sony's November 23 PlayStation Blog announcement reports more than 39 million distinct PS5 players and 683 million cumulative gameplay hours during the November 19–22 launch period. These are PlayStation engagement figures, not global sales or an online player count.

The numbers help explain why a future shared-world experience attracts so much attention. A huge story audience could become a multiplayer audience, but one cannot predict that conversion rate or assume everybody who played the story will join online. Sony's figures establish the launch scale, not a multiplayer service's availability.

There is plenty to explore in story mode without relying on a multiplayer announcement. Familiarize yourself with the map, driving controls, accessibility settings and the campaign's mechanics. Keep the console version up to date through its normal update process.
