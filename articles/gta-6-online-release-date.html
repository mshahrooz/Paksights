When Is GTA 6 Online Coming Out? Multiplayer Release Date and 2027 Rumors

Five days after Grand Theft Auto VI arrived, players are asking when they can explore Leonida together. Here's what we know, what we don't, and why the 2027 rumor is not a release date.

Quick answer: no confirmed GTA VI Online release date.

Rockstar launched GTA VI's single-player campaign on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. As of November 24, the developer has not announced a multiplayer release date.

Is GTA 6 Online out now?

No official GTA VI multiplayer mode has been announced as available. Rockstar's launch announcement confirms the two console platforms. A new multiplayer experience, however eagerly anticipated, has a separate set of unanswered questions.

Connecting a console to the internet to install updates or use account services is not the same as entering an online multiplayer world. Do not confuse the existing GTA Online, which belongs to GTA V, with a new service for GTA VI.

Will GTA 6 Online come out in 2027?

The Independent's November 24 report highlights an earlier suggestion by Twitch CEO Dan Clancy that multiplayer might follow next year. The Sun also covered the question today. These reports are evidence of renewed interest, not official confirmation of a launch window.

Clancy's remarks were about anticipated streaming interest, not a Rockstar announcement. A predicted year cannot tell players when preloading, beta tests or public matchmaking will begin. Even if 2027 ultimately proves correct, a specific date circulating on social media would remain unverified until the developer announces it.

Our view: the distinction matters because people can make purchasing decisions around an assumed launch. Anyone buying a console primarily to play multiplayer with friends should wait for the feature and platform details.

What happened with GTA V and the original GTA Online?

GTA V launched on September 17, 2013, while the original GTA Online opened on October 1, 2013. That historical gap shows Rockstar can separate story mode and multiplayer. It does not establish a two-week schedule for the new game.

It would be misleading to add fourteen days to November 19 and label the result GTA VI Online's official release date. The new game's scale and platform ecosystem are different, and Rockstar has not announced its multiplayer timetable.

For now, avoid buying currency in the existing GTA Online expecting it to transfer to a new game. A shared platform, familiar map or returning character does not guarantee shared progression. Crossplay should be treated the same way: wait for an explicit announcement.

Why multiplayer is such a big post-launch question

On November 23, PlayStation's official blog declared GTA VI its biggest launch to date, reporting over 39 million unique PS5 players and 683 million cumulative hours of gameplay. That is a remarkable audience for the story, not evidence that GTA VI Online is already operating.
