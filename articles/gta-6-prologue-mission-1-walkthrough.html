01 / STORY SETUP

What happens in GTA 6's first mission?

Grand Theft Auto VI opens with the Prologue, a story-first introduction centering on Lucia Caminos. Its opening sequence gives context for her relationship with Jason before gameplay puts you inside Hamlet prison. The playable tutorial follows Lucia from her cell to a counselor's appointment, then takes you into a flashback that explains the robbery which landed her behind bars.

The mission is a carefully directed sequence, not an open-ended heist. You learn how to interact with people, follow an escort, threaten a counter clerk, handle a struggle and read a police search area on the minimap. The ending is part of the scripted story, even if you try to outrun the police.

02 / STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

GTA VI Prologue walkthrough: all objectives

Below is a step-by-step explanation based on the individually documented PowerPyx walkthrough, checked against VGC and GamesRadar+. Game prompts take priority if your interface wording differs.

MISSION CHECKLIST
  1. Talk to your cellmates.
  2. Follow the prison guard to the counselor.
  3. Threaten the payday-loan teller and take the money.
  4. Flee the police, return to the coffee shop and reach the scripted finale.
01 / 07

Lucia's prison cell

After the opening cinematic, the first playable character is Lucia Caminos. The game places her by a barred window in Hamlet prison and introduces basic movement and the contextual conversation prompt. Interact with cellmates, but don't worry about missing a hidden objective here.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
02 / 07

Follow CO Wilson

Correctional officer Wilson opens the cell and escorts Lucia out. Stay near him through the common area, wait while he stops to use the phone, then follow him down the corridor. The path is scripted, so there is no need to search for a different exit.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
03 / 07

Meet counselor Stefanie Gates

The interview centers on Lucia's arrest and possible early release. The counselor's questions provide the narrative bridge to events months earlier. This is a story scene rather than a choice-heavy branch; let the conversation run.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
04 / 07

The payday-loan robbery flashback

The flashback puts Lucia outside a coffee shop before moving inside a Payday Loan / check-cashing counter. Aim at the teller and use the on-screen threat interaction; wait for the money to be placed where it can be collected, and follow the pickup prompt. Don't substitute a GTA V button combination: use the prompt shown for your platform.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
05 / 07

Break free and flee

A customer tries to restrain Lucia after she gets the cash. Complete the quick-time interaction, then move away from the store. Police detection is shown on the minimap. Treat this as a directed story escape rather than an open-world wanted-level challenge.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
06 / 07

Return to the coffee shop

The sequence returns to the counselor's office and then resumes the flashback. When control comes back, head toward the coffee shop marker. Lucia's arrest is part of the story and cannot be permanently avoided by running farther away.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
07 / 07

The Prologue closes

The final conversation explains why Lucia returned, adding stakes to her wish for release. Control then moves toward Jason's story, with Gone Fishing appearing next in published walkthrough orders. The Prologue is not a free-roam challenge with a separate cash reward.

SOURCE WALKTHROUGH ↗
REVEAL A MAJOR ENDING-OF-PROLOGUE STORY DETAIL +

The closing dialogue reveals that Lucia returned to the coffee shop for her baby, Tommy, which contributes to the police finding and arresting her. This is why the final part of the mission should not be treated as a winnable escape challenge.

05 / PLAYER TIPS & TROUBLESHOOTING

How to finish the Prologue without getting stuck

01

Stay with the escort

Wait at the guard's brief stop, then follow him into the next corridor. Don't wander into unmarked cell areas.

02

Read the teller prompt

Aim at the clerk and hold the prompted threaten interaction until the cash is available. Picking up money happens afterward.

03

Complete the struggle QTE

The bystander interaction is a short scripted struggle. Follow the on-screen button prompts instead of attempting a free-aim shootout.

04

Don't fight the story arrest

Running indefinitely cannot change the required narrative outcome. Follow the marker back to the coffee shop when control returns.

06 / FAQ

Questions about GTA VI Mission 1

What is GTA 6 Mission 1 called? +

The opening playable story mission is Prologue, featuring Lucia Caminos. Some video playlists separately number Gone Fishing as Mission #1 of Chapter 1 because the Prologue stands apart from the first full chapter.

Who do you play as in the GTA VI Prologue? +

Lucia Caminos is the playable lead during the prison interview and robbery flashback. Jason appears in opening narrative material and becomes playable in the following mission.

Where does the first mission take place? +

The Prologue begins in Hamlet prison and includes a flashback around a Vice City coffee shop and payday-loan counter.

Can you escape the police in GTA 6 Prologue? +

The arrest is scripted as part of the story. Guides describe a brief chase segment but agree that Lucia cannot permanently avoid being taken into custody.

Does the Prologue reward money or a gold medal? +

PowerPyx lists no mission reward; its launch walkthrough also reports no GTA VI mission gold-medal feature.

How long is GTA 6 Mission 1? +

A dedicated GTA Series Videos walkthrough runs approximately 17 minutes and 48 seconds including introductory moments. A player's pace and time in cutscenes can differ.

Does this article contain real screenshots of the first mission? +

Its gallery combines official Rockstar promotional and trailer frames directly related to Lucia's prison setting with clearly marked thumbnails from genuine recorded first-mission walkthroughs. Trailer images are not mislabeled as playable gameplay captures.
