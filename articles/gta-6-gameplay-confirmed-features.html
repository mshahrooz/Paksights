GTA 6 gameplay: the features we can actually verify.

A careful look at official gameplay footage and Rockstar’s documented control categories.

VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ The strongest evidence: direct in-game footage

Rockstar published an extended look described as captured entirely from in-game PlayStation 5 footage. That provides an unusually substantial source for evaluating the look and pace of the world. It does not grant permission to assume every environmental detail is interactive.

02/ Documented movement and vehicle categories

Rockstar's settings documentation includes on-foot controls, aiming, melee, vehicles, aircraft, bicycles, swimming and parachuting. These categories establish supported control families. They are more reliable than viral posts claiming dozens of hidden mechanics.

03/ A broad world without made-up systems

Officially shown locations range from Vice City to wetlands, island chains and national-park scenery. Each environment adds texture to Leonida. Whether a given scene is free-roam, scripted, optional or mission-bound requires more than a trailer frame to establish.

04/ Accessibility belongs in gameplay coverage

The studio's settings guide mentions UI scaling, high-contrast shaders, subtitles, narration and camera-comfort options. These are concrete gameplay-adjacent features that can affect the experience every day, and we give them dedicated coverage rather than leaving them in a footnote.

05/ Our verification standard

We mark a claim confirmed only when Rockstar documents it or shows it clearly. For player-discovered systems, we would require replicable in-game observation. This is especially important for inventory, police behavior, property systems, online play and other topics where speculation spreads faster than evidence.
