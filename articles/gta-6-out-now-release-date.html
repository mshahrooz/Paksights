Rockstar Games confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here's the source-checked launch briefing, without the viral noise.
✓
VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ The release date is no longer a rumor

Rockstar’s November 19 news post explicitly states that GTA VI is available worldwide. That supersedes older search snippets saying “coming soon,” which were accurate before launch but are now stale. Our release tracker uses the dated publisher announcement as its primary record.

The game lands more than a decade after Grand Theft Auto V. Rather than speculate about future content, our launch coverage concentrates on what players can buy, where they can play, and which first-party answers exist today.

02/ Which systems can play GTA VI?

The native launch platforms named by Rockstar are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A native Windows PC version and a release date for it have not been announced in the referenced official platform guidance. Cloud and Remote Play features are separate from a PC release.

If you are shopping for the game, confirm your console family and store region before purchasing, especially if buying a code-in-box retail edition.

03/ A new state, two protagonists

The setting is Leonida, including Vice City and surrounding regions. Rockstar's official materials introduce Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the leading characters. Promotional descriptions suggest an intertwined story, but this article deliberately avoids plot spoilers.

04/ Three useful things to do next

Read our platforms guide before buying; check the edition comparison for US pricing and the unusual physical-packaging detail; and keep the beginner settings article handy for accessibility and control options. Each guide links back to original Rockstar documentation.

Rockstar also asked players to be considerate about story spoilers. You can browse Leonida Ledger’s launch coverage with the same spoiler-light approach.
