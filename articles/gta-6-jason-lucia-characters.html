VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos

Jason and Lucia drive the story described by Rockstar's promotional material. Their partnership and the pressure around them are foregrounded in the official trailers. The studio’s own biographies provide the grounded introduction; fan-written plot summaries are not substitutes for official material.

Because the game is now live, unmarked story spoilers can circulate quickly. Our character coverage uses the public promotional setup rather than late-game revelations.

02/ Cal Hampton and Brian Heder

Cal Hampton and Brian Heder are both named on Rockstar’s official characters and locations pages. The existence of a profile confirms a character, not every fan theory about their ultimate allegiance or fate.

03/ Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest and Real Dimez

These names also appear in Rockstar's world material, helping establish the wider music and entertainment milieu around Vice City. Treat personality details and relationships beyond the official profiles as interpretive until directly supported.

04/ Raul Bautista and the larger world

Raul Bautista is another named character. With this many introduced figures, GTA VI's story world is evidently larger than just the lead duo, but exact mission participation and endings are not topics for a spoiler-light launch guide.

05/ Where to see the original profiles

Use Rockstar's Only in Leonida portal and Trailer 2. Our roster is designed as a reference for names and official introductions, not a fan wiki that guesses at story outcomes.
