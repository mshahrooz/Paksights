Four major official video releases tell the public story of GTA VI before launch. This timeline uses publisher uploads, not fan-made edits marketed as ‘Trailer 3’.
✓
VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ December 2023: Trailer 1 introduces Leonida

Rockstar’s first GTA VI trailer established the return to Vice City and introduced Lucia to a mass audience. It sold the mood and setting more than it explained systems. When revisiting this trailer, focus on the imagery it actually shows rather than predictions made from single-frame backgrounds.

02/ May 6, 2025: Trailer 2 centers Jason and Lucia

Trailer 2 gives the central pair more narrative context. Rockstar has described the video as captured from an in-game PlayStation 5 build, combining gameplay and cinematics. That does not mean every moment is footage of uninterrupted player control.

03/ August 27, 2026: An Extended Look

The roughly 27-minute official extended look is the most substantial pre-launch showcase of the game’s world and presentation. Rockstar specifically identified PlayStation 5 in-game capture. For feature analysis, it is stronger evidence than commentary or leaks because viewers can inspect the footage directly.

04/ October 15, 2026: the launch trailer

The launch trailer was the final major marketing beat ahead of the November 19 release. It is valuable context for the game's tone, music and visual presentation, but its scenes are still curated marketing material.

05/ Watch without spoilers

Start with Trailer 1 and 2, then the extended presentation, then the launch trailer. All four can be found from Rockstar’s official video pages. We intentionally link to the originals rather than reposting videos as our own.
