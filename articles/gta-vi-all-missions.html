Looking for every GTA VI mission? This guide indexes the 91 core story missions in the five-chapter ordering published by PC Gamer, adds two Chapter 2 missions sometimes included in 93-mission lists, and explains why one other guide counts 95. Each chapter has concise, source-backed context; important missions get extra progression and walkthrough notes.

Mission titles themselves can spoil story beats. Chapters 3–5 are folded closed until you choose to open them. The original Rockstar illustrations on this page are promotional screenshots, not purported images from each individual mission.

SPOILER SETTINGS

The full story list contains meaningful mission-title spoilers. Chapters 3–5 start folded closed. You can reveal them individually or use the controls below.

01 / MISSION COUNT EXPLAINED

How many missions are in GTA VI?

The most useful number depends on what you are counting. PC Gamer separates the principal narrative missions and reports 91: 22 in Chapter 1, 11 in Chapter 2, 26 in Chapter 3, 25 in Chapter 4 and 7 in Chapter 5. Push Square prints 93 entries, counting two jobs PowerPyx marks as optional. VGC prints 95 names from the game's story recap, including further differently classified tasks. None of those totals should be presented as a Rockstar-published universal count.

07 / BEYOND THE CORE STORY

Optional and missable missions

Not every pink or purple activity on the map is part of the 91-core narrative. The title Thrillbillies is tied to the Mud Club, and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab follows Billy's work. Both appear in the 93-entry chapter count, but PowerPyx labels them optional Chapter 2 missions. Their inclusion explains two extra entries in several lists.

CHAPTER 2 / OPTIONAL

Thrillbillies

Visit the Mud Club and follow Billy's first mission strand; not required for advancing the core storyline under the 91-mission convention.

CHAPTER 2 / OPTIONAL

Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab

Join Billy for the next related job. Some websites count it among the principal chapter titles even though other guides mark it optional.

Other walkthrough-documented side activities include Shirley's two Chapter 1 favors (Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter), Kyle's jobs, Yago's race-related work, an optional Chapter 4 phone pickup and Tyrone's lab strand. Some are time-sensitive. PowerPyx says the game's hourglass marker warns about optional work that can disappear, while the main plot remains playable.

Why some lists show 95 or even 120-plus tasks: the pause-menu story tracker can also show brief milestones such as a required payment, a call, gathering money or heist preparation. Those are real in-game objectives, but treating every small objective as an independent full mission changes the total. If you want the expanded task-by-task form, use the PowerPyx directory rather than interpreting 91 as a total of every possible activity.

OTHER REPORTED ACTIVITIES

VGC also includes names such as Kitty Litter Labs and Spin on This within its longer chapter-3 presentation. PowerPyx instead discusses the Tyrone laboratory strand among side activities. This is a classification difference, not evidence that those activities are nonexistent.

08 / PROGRESSION ADVICE

How to make progress without missing content

01

Use the map's story markers

PowerPyx describes main story markers as pink with black letters; optional missions use the opposite color treatment and smaller tasks may appear in purple. Use the map's story-book menu to review current objectives.

02

Don't expect a strict linear order

Several quest givers become available at once. If a marker has not appeared, complete another available assignment or advance in-game time, rather than assuming the guide is missing a mission.

03

Preserve manual save points

PowerPyx reports that the launch version lacks a mission-replay option and gold-medal challenges. A save near chapter transitions gives you a route back if you want to revisit a section.

04

Separate story from 100%

Finishing the story does not finish every collectible, activity or completion requirement. The full in-game completion list opens after the story according to detailed trophy guides.

For a private checklist where you can add missions at your own pace, open the free GTA VI mission tracker. It does not upload your progress or force late-game titles onto the screen.

09 / QUICK ANSWERS

GTA VI mission FAQ

How many story missions are in GTA VI? +

PC Gamer lists 91 core story missions. Push Square lists 93 and VGC lists 95 because outlets include or classify additional optional tasks differently. Rockstar has not published one reconciled count.

How many chapters are in GTA 6? +

Five story chapters: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, Tits Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby and Top of the World, Ma.

What is the first GTA VI mission? +

Prologue, featuring Lucia at Hamlet Prison and a flashback sequence.

What is the final GTA 6 story mission? +

The End of the Road is the title identified by multiple published walkthroughs. We avoid revealing the outcome.

Can you replay GTA VI missions? +

At launch, PowerPyx reports no built-in mission replay. Keep manual saves at major story transitions if you want to revisit sections.

Are the GTA VI optional missions needed for 100% completion? +

PowerPyx reports certain time-sensitive optional missions are missable but do not block trophies or 100% completion. Other completion requirements exist, so finishing just the core story is not equivalent to 100%.

How much money do you need to leave Port Gellhorn? +

The Prepare for Vice City objective in Chapter 2 requires at least $15,000 before Checking Out becomes available.
