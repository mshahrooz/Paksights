GTA 6 accessibility options: a practical checklist for players.

What Rockstar documents about contrast, narration, captions and camera comfort, plus what to inspect yourself.

GTA VI's support documentation describes several options for readability, narration and comfort. Here is a quick, non-technical guide to finding the right starting point.
✓
VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ Text size and subtitles

If dialogue is hard to read from the couch, check subtitle and UI scaling options. Rockstar lists both among the settings categories. Try a few sizes at your normal distance rather than deciding by how the menu looks close to the screen.

02/ High contrast and visual differentiation

Rockstar documents high-contrast shaders. Explore them when environmental contrast makes a scene difficult to parse. Be aware that an option's usefulness is individual; the existence of the setting does not guarantee a specific accessibility outcome.

03/ Narration and camera comfort

Narration support and camera-comfort options are both named in Rockstar's support material. Test changes in a quiet setting before a demanding mission. Use accessible controls in the manner that works for your own needs.

04/ Controller flexibility

The official settings guide also includes sensitivity, inverted controls and platform-specific controller features. If a default layout is uncomfortable, inspect the available adjustments and note down what you change.

05/ A transparency note

This article is a documentation-based overview, not a laboratory accessibility review. We have not tested every option across all platforms and displays; details and effectiveness may vary with updates.
