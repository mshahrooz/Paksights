SUNDAY EDITION · NOVEMBER 22, 2026 · SPOILER-LIGHT REPORTING

Three days after Grand Theft Auto VI arrived, the news is no longer about waiting for a release date. It's about scale: billions in reported launch sales, massive download traffic, extraordinary critical acclaim—and the first wave of real-world questions from players. Here is the detailed, source-checked GTA 6 briefing for November 22.

01/ GTA VI has already generated a staggering $3.79 billion

The headline dominating the GTA 6 conversation is straightforward: on Friday, November 20, Take-Two Interactive announced that the game's estimated worldwide day-one retail sales reached $3.79 billion. The publisher calls it the largest entertainment launch across any medium. Its estimate covers sales through the end of November 19, the official release day.

Take-Two also said 32.5 million units were sold before release, a separate record it described as pre-release sales. That helps explain why the game's first 24-hour number is so extraordinary. Those figures should not be added together as though they represent independent revenue streams: pre-release purchases and launch-period accounting are related, and the source does not publish a complete consumer-by-consumer breakdown.

For context, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto V in 2013, meaning fans waited roughly 13 years for a mainline successor. However, the $3.79 billion figure is retail sales, not Take-Two's net profit. Development costs, digital storefront fees, distribution arrangements, taxes, refunds and accounting rules mean simplistic social-media profit calculations are unreliable.

Read Take-Two's original sales announcement ↗ or compare Reuters' independent reporting ↗. The distinction matters: the press release contains company estimates, while Reuters reports and contextualizes that claim.

02/ GTA 6 did not merely trend—it transformed internet traffic

Another remarkable story comes directly from broadband provider Comcast. The company said November 18 was the busiest day in its network's history and that GTA VI-related activity helped drive its peak internet traffic 31% above an average day. At the busiest moments of the launch period, GTA VI accounted for as much as 24% of traffic on Comcast's network.

These statistics are not measurements of every internet provider in the world, nor do they mean 24% of all internet activity everywhere was GTA 6. They're a striking picture of one giant U.S. network reacting to preloads, installs, updates and launch-related traffic. Downloads also overlap with ordinary activities, including streaming and work, so the precise composition of everyone's traffic varies throughout the day.

24% OF NETWORK TRAFFIC. ONE GAME.

Comcast says GTA VI reached this share at peak periods on its own network. Read the original Comcast network report for the full context.

For new players, the practical takeaway is mundane but important: storage space, bandwidth caps, system updates and download queues are part of an event this large. Preloading meant some of the traffic burst happened before the public launch day itself.

03/ Critics praise GTA VI—but the player conversation is more complicated

Take-Two's November 20 release highlighted a 99 Metacritic critic score for the game. Major reviews published as the embargo lifted were enthusiastic about the detail of Leonida, the writing, and the relationship between protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. The Guardian's review, for example, focused on the extraordinary density of Rockstar's world and the central love story.

But critical acclaim and player satisfaction are not interchangeable. Early user-score snapshots have been notably lower than the critic aggregate, and forums have discussed frame rates, bugs, pricing and whether console performance meets expectations. The score of a crowd-rated platform can shift quickly; it also doesn't independently establish the motives behind every rating. Calling all negative reviews 'review bombing' without evidence is not a responsible shortcut.

The more useful question is not whether the game is universally perfect, but what matters to you: story pacing, exploration, driving, accessibility, input response or performance. Read the Guardian's full review ↗ and compare it with the current Metacritic listings, checking the date and platform of each score.

04/ Today's discovery: an Xbox glitch appears to unlock higher frame rates

One of November 22's freshest GTA 6 stories involves a reported Xbox Series X frame-rate workaround. Players and gaming outlets describe a bug involving high-refresh-rate output and Xbox Quick Resume that can apparently push the game beyond its intended console frame-rate limit.

There is a major catch: coverage from GamesRadar+ and other outlets says the game may run at accelerated speed, with animations and world simulation moving unnaturally fast. That is not equivalent to a stable, properly timed official 60 fps mode, and the trick may vary by console, game version and display settings.

FACT CHECK: NOT AN OFFICIAL 60 FPS PATCH

As of this November 22 edition, the reported Xbox behavior should be treated as an unofficial glitch, not a Rockstar-supported mode or proof of an upcoming performance patch. Avoid circulating clips with incorrect claims about native 60 fps gameplay. Read today's GamesRadar+ report ↗

If you prefer to play without technical experiments, the sensible approach is to use the game's standard display settings and watch Rockstar Support or its official news channels for verified performance updates. No particular patch date is confirmed by the cited report.

05/ The soundtrack is out, and Vice City has taken over Miami

The celebration is bigger than the game itself. On November 20, Rockstar said Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album was available, featuring 34 original songs and contributions from artists including Playboi Carti, Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, Don Toliver, JENNIE and others. It's a real, announced release—not a fan-made playlist.

Rockstar has also announced a separate original score composed by French electronic duo Justice. The score album is set to arrive on December 11, 2026, with 30 tracks announced for the main album. Don't confuse the already released collection of original songs with that later instrumental score release.

Another major launch-weekend event was Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, scheduled for Saturday, November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami. Rockstar described it as a live celebration of music from Vice City and Leonida. A festival taking place is not itself confirmation of a gameplay announcement, a PC release or a new GTA Online launch date.

07/ What has not been announced: PC and a new GTA Online date

The first weekend's viral speculation can easily overshadow two simple facts. First, Rockstar's official launch announcement names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. As of November 22, the publisher has not provided a PC release date on its current GTA VI page. Claims naming a specific future month should therefore be labeled speculation unless accompanied by a dated official announcement.

Second, Rockstar has not announced a confirmed launch date for a new GTA VI multiplayer/Online experience in the primary news posts reviewed for this report. This does not mean the company has ruled out multiplayer. It means there's a difference between the existing GTA Online service associated with GTA V and future GTA VI-specific online plans.

Look for announcements on Rockstar's GTA VI Newswire ↗ instead of treating a screenshot, alleged insider schedule or festival rumor as a launch confirmation. We will update the article if new verified information appears.

08/ GTA VI questions people are asking today

Is GTA 6 officially out today?

Yes. Rockstar released the game globally for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026.

How much money did GTA 6 make in the first 24 hours?

Take-Two's November 20 press release estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide day-one retail sales through November 19. That is a company estimate, not a reported profit figure.

Did GTA 6 really account for 24% of internet traffic?

Comcast said GTA VI activity made up as much as 24% of its own network traffic at peak periods. The claim does not describe the whole global internet.

Can GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on Xbox?

A November 22 report describes an unofficial Xbox glitch that appears to unlock frame rates but can make gameplay run at an unnaturally high speed. It isn't a supported native performance mode.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has not announced a confirmed PC release date as of November 22, 2026. Check official Rockstar channels before accepting a specific date.

When will GTA 6 Online launch?

No verified release date for a GTA VI-specific online mode has been published in the Rockstar announcements reviewed for this report.
