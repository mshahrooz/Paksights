GTA 6 map guide: six regions of Leonida Rockstar has revealed.

From Vice City’s skyline to the Leonida Keys, discover the confirmed geography without invented map measurements.

Leonida is more than Vice City. Rockstar’s official world guide names six distinct areas, each with its own atmosphere. This guide separates those named locations from speculative map-scale claims.
✓
VERIFIED SOURCE POLICY

Claims below are anchored to Rockstar, Take-Two or documented platform information. Our interpretations are clearly distinguished from official statements.

01/ Vice City: the neon anchor

Vice City is the recognizable urban heart of GTA VI’s setting. Rockstar’s art shows coastal boulevards, busy nightlife and modern high-rises. It is tempting to treat every frame as evidence of a specific route or activity, but screenshots alone cannot confirm a mission path or full street map.

02/ Leonida Keys: water, bridges and islands

The Keys bring an island-and-causeway visual identity, with turquoise water and boats highlighted in official images. They are a natural focus for a scenery guide, not a reason to invent a definitive list of available watercraft or underwater activities.

03/ Grassrivers: wetlands and wildlife imagery

Rockstar presents Grassrivers as a contrasting landscape of waterways and marshes. Details visible in trailers can be described as visuals; mechanics such as specific wildlife interactions should be separately verified before being labeled features.

04/ Port Gellhorn: a different coastal energy

Port Gellhorn is another named area in Leonida. Its official imagery moves away from glossy downtown scenes and helps explain how the game's regional identity extends beyond postcard views of Vice City.

05/ Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park

Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park round out the six named areas. One suggests a more industrial or inland part of Leonida; the other represents a natural landscape. Rockstar’s own regional pages remain the most useful starting point for visual exploration.

06/ What the screenshots cannot prove

No amount of screenshot stitching is an official statement of total square mileage, travel time or every accessible interior. Maps made by fans may be interesting hypotheses, but they are not a substitute for publisher-provided geography. We label speculative cartography as such.
