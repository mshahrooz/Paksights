ABOUT LEONIDA LEDGER

An independent publication for people who want real GTA VI information without rumor overload.

Leonida Ledger is an unofficial editorial project published on PakSights. We cover Grand Theft Auto VI with a simple principle: facts first, hype second.

What we cover

Our reporting focuses on dated release information, platform availability, buying choices, official videos, confirmed characters, places in Leonida, and useful support documentation. We avoid pretending to have tested features we have not tested.

Who writes it?

Launch articles are prepared by the Leonida Ledger Research Desk, an editorial byline for source-based research and writing. It is not a claim that we are Rockstar employees, game developers, or hands-on reviewers. We clearly identify first-party documentation where relevant.

Is this an official Rockstar site?

No. Leonida Ledger is not owned, operated, sponsored or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA VI, related names and game imagery belong to their respective rights holders.

How do we pay for publishing?

We may display Google AdSense advertisements. Ads are not endorsements and do not influence editorial conclusions. Read our privacy and advertising policy and editorial standards.
