How to file an overseas Pakistani complaint with OPF

Find the correct channel for property, document, fraud and administrative grievances.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

OPF maintains a complaint cell and Online Complaint Management System to coordinate grievances from overseas Pakistanis with relevant Pakistani departments. According to OPF’s February 2026 notice, its online complaint process is free.

What you should know first

A complaint should make one request to one relevant authority clear. OPF’s role is to coordinate and follow up, not to issue court judgments or guarantee relief.

OPF lists proof of identity and overseas status, supporting documents and a focal contact in Pakistan among its complaint requirements.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Identify the subject and existing authority

Decide whether your issue concerns land, an agency, a document, an employer or a bank. Use the primary regulator first where appropriate.

02

Organize documents chronologically

Create a concise account of dates, events, previous complaints and desired outcome. Keep original supporting papers.

03

Check the official OPF route

Start from opf.org.pk and follow the Complaint Cell / OCMS instructions to the official complaint system.

04

Submit your identity and evidence

Provide required identification, overseas-status proof and documents; avoid unnecessary unrelated personal material.

05

Record your complaint number

Save acknowledgments and allow the responsible department time to respond.

06

Escalate safely

If you suspect fraud by someone claiming to be from OPF, use OPF’s published official contacts; the agency says it does not demand payment for complaint processing.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • OPF says its complaint portal is free: never pay an “expediting fee”.
  • OPF facilitation is not legal representation and does not stop a court deadline.
  • Avoid duplicate or contradictory submissions with missing evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Who can complain?

OPF describes services for eligible overseas Pakistanis and related authorized applicants; check its requirements.

Are OPF complaint fees payable?

OPF’s 2026 notice explicitly says online complaint registration and processing are free.

Can I raise a property problem?

Yes. OPF lists property disputes among matters it can coordinate.
