HEC degree attestation: the online process for overseas applicants

A clear route through the new e-attestation system and common document problems.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

HEC describes a fully online, paperless degree-attestation system for eligible Pakistani higher-education credentials. The published steps are online application, HEC scrutiny, university verification and issuance of an electronic attestation certificate.

What you should know first

Some older online guides still describe physically submitting original degrees by courier or at a counter. HEC’s current attestation page states that its new system issues e-attestation and does not require hard-copy submissions for this workflow.

This distinction matters when someone abroad is about to pay an agent. Use HEC’s actual portal rather than websites that imitate government branding.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Establish which service you need

Attestation of a Pakistani degree is different from equivalence of a foreign degree; HEC says foreign credentials are not directly attested as Pakistani degrees.

02

Check record consistency

Make sure name, ID, degree, transcript and institution details are accurate. Correct institutional errors before applying.

03

Open the official HEC e-services portal

Access it from HEC’s degree-attestation page and sign in securely.

04

Submit the required records

Provide the degree, transcript and other evidence requested by the application.

05

Wait for HEC and university verification

Track scrutiny and awarding-university authentication in the system.

06

Download and verify your certificate

Retrieve the e-attestation certificate after issuance and follow the foreign recipient’s rules for additional authentication, if any.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • HEC warns that fake websites and callers impersonate its attestation services.
  • A foreign university degree may require an equivalence process instead.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords or bank details with a supposed HEC representative calling you.

Frequently asked questions

Must I courier original degrees to HEC?

HEC’s current degree-attestation page describes a fully online paperless system. Follow its latest instructions for your case.

Does HEC attest a foreign university degree?

HEC says it issues foreign-degree equivalence letters rather than directly attesting foreign degrees.

Which portal is genuine?

HEC identifies eservices.hec.gov.pk as its official service portal.
