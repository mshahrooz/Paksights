Renew a Pakistani passport online from overseas

What DGI&P says about eligibility, timing and the online renewal journey.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

The Directorate General of Immigration & Passports offers online passport renewal to inland and overseas Pakistanis. Its guidance says to apply when the passport has expired or has less than one year of validity remaining.

What you should know first

Passport renewal and replacement after loss, damage or a change in particulars are not necessarily the same workflow. Starting with the right service can save a repeat application.

The passport portal warns that online fees once paid are non-refundable and non-transferable. Review your entries and selected service before paying, and check your destination’s entry rules separately.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Check the renewal condition

Review the online renewal page and ensure your passport is expired or within the renewal window described by DGI&P.

02

Match identity records

Compare passport name, date of birth and identity number with your NADRA documents. Resolve discrepancies through the appropriate authority.

03

Start on DGI&P’s site

Follow the Apply for Online Passport Renewal link from the official DGI&P e-services page.

04

Submit required details and images

Complete the form, upload the evidence requested for your case and follow instructions for photo or biometric data.

05

Verify fees and submission

Check service category, address and all details carefully. DGI&P warns against expecting a refund or fee transfer.

06

Plan travel conservatively

Keep confirmation details and leave flexibility around flight plans; avoid assuming a promised delivery date is guaranteed.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • A visa or residence permit can have different passport-validity rules from Pakistan’s renewal criteria.
  • Lost or damaged passports may require a separate route or consular assistance.
  • Only use passport links reached from the official DGI&P domain.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply without visiting Pakistan?

DGI&P says online renewal is available to overseas Pakistanis; eligibility and additional steps depend on the case.

Is the fee refundable if I make a mistake?

DGI&P states an online passport fee, once paid, is non-refundable and non-transferable.

How early can I renew?

DGI&P’s published online renewal guidance references expired passports or validity of less than one year.
