Packing for Pakistan in December: start with your cities

A winter checklist for travellers visiting different cities and climates in Pakistan.

PakSights editorial desk · Published 11 November 2026 · Research checked 11 November 2026

In brief: A winter checklist for travellers visiting different cities and climates in Pakistan. Use the sections below as a flexible planning framework and check official information before making decisions.

Winter conditions vary

The weather in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and higher-altitude areas is not the same. Check the forecast for each stop shortly before departure and update clothing choices accordingly. Do not assume that a single warm jacket is enough for every destination.

Use layers instead of bulky duplicates

Bring breathable clothing, comfortable shoes, socks, an optional warm mid-layer and outerwear suited to the actual forecast. Ask relatives whether the home has heating, warm bedding and laundry facilities; indoor comfort can be as important as daytime temperatures.

Pack for the practical parts

Keep moisturizer, a small rain layer if forecast, essential medication, a spare change in cabin baggage and weather-appropriate sleepwear. If visiting mountains, research the route, altitude and road conditions as a separate decision.

Leave room to return

Gifts and purchases often use baggage space. Weigh bags after packing and check the most restrictive carrier on connecting flights. Follow airline rules on batteries, liquids and prohibited articles.

Keep this checklist handy

Weather in every destination; heating and bedding at home; laundry access; one complete cabin change; shoes; layering; return-flight baggage allowance.

Frequently asked question

Do we need a heavy coat everywhere?

Not necessarily. Base your choice on destinations, current forecasts and accommodation.
