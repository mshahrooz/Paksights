Checking property records in Sindh from abroad

What Sindh Zameen provides—and why you still need certified documents.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

Sindh’s Board of Revenue provides the Sindh Zameen public portal for searching digital land-record information, including village forms and certain registry-related material. The portal itself cautions that online information is for public information and has no legal validity.

What you should know first

The first job is to determine which body maintains the relevant record. Agricultural revenue land, a privately developed housing society and a city authority plot may require different records and approval checks.

Sindh Zameen provides search features and maps, but explains that users should obtain official certified documents from a Revenue Service Center. An online result is a useful lead, not a title certificate.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Identify the area and record type

Gather district, deh, land details or available registration information.

02

Use Sindh’s government portal

Access sindhzameen.gos.pk and try the applicable name, identity or registry search feature offered by the portal.

03

Record exact matches and limitations

Confirm the spelling, parcel information and record type. If the site says no match, do not assume ownership is clear or impossible.

04

Request certified copies

Follow the Board of Revenue directions for obtaining legally usable records from an authorized service centre.

05

Get independent title review

A lawyer should review the sale deed, title chain, registered encumbrances, disputes, permissions and the correct authority’s records.

06

Confirm the transfer process

Ask the competent registry or society which taxes, signatures and approvals are required before making payment.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • Sindh Zameen expressly says portal information has no legal validity.
  • Do not mistake a public search result for a certified title document.
  • Marketing brochures are not proof of legal approvals.

Frequently asked questions

Can I search the Sindh database online?

Yes. The official Board of Revenue portal offers public search facilities for digitized records.

Is online data legally conclusive?

No. The portal advises obtaining certified documents from the competent office.

What if the property is in a housing society?

Check the issuing society or development authority and the applicable land-record authority separately.
