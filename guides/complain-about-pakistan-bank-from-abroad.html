How to complain about a Pakistani bank from overseas

Build an evidence trail and use the State Bank of Pakistan’s Sunwai service.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

Sunwai is an SBP-backed online complaint platform for issues involving banks, microfinance banks and development financial institutions, including Roshan Digital Accounts.

What you should know first

The Sunwai portal routes complaints to the appropriate forum, including the financial institution as the first forum and Banking Mohtasib or SBP in relevant cases. This is more effective than sending repeated unstructured messages to unrelated offices.

The quality of your evidence matters. Make your complaint easy to understand by specifying the transaction, what went wrong, what you want resolved and what you have already tried.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Put account security first

For unauthorized transactions or suspected fraud, use your bank’s official fraud hotline immediately, not just a slower general complaint.

02

Write a timeline

List dates, branch or channel, the transaction reference, monetary amount and what was promised. Avoid adding unrelated accusations.

03

Contact the bank

Use the bank’s published complaint channel and get a case/reference number. Preserve the response and applicable deadlines.

04

Open Sunwai

Use sunwai.sbp.org.pk. Register securely, follow the questionnaire and select the applicable complaint route.

05

Attach supporting documents

Provide redacted bank statements, email screenshots, case numbers and other relevant records without sharing passwords or OTPs.

06

Track and escalate appropriately

Keep the Sunwai tracking number and respond to information requests. Follow the portal’s routing instructions before escalating elsewhere.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • Sunwai is a complaint-routing system, not a guarantee of compensation.
  • Never share an OTP or full password in an email or portal attachment.
  • The correct escalation forum can depend on complaint type and institution.

Frequently asked questions

Can I file a Sunwai complaint from abroad?

The portal is web-based; check its identification and contact requirements when registering.

Does Sunwai cover Roshan Digital Accounts?

Yes. SBP specifically includes RDA-related banking complaints.

Should I go directly to Banking Mohtasib?

Sunwai explains and routes the appropriate sequence; typically the bank is the first forum.
