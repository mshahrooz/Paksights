NICOP vs POC: which card should you apply for?

Understand the citizenship distinction before paying for the wrong NADRA document.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

NICOP is for Pakistani citizens living overseas. POC is generally for eligible foreign nationals of Pakistani origin. Your citizenship status—not simply where you live—drives the choice.

What you should know first

NICOP and Pakistan Origin Card (POC) may look like similar overseas identity products, but NADRA defines them differently. A person who still holds Pakistani citizenship should not automatically choose a POC because they also hold a foreign passport.

NADRA lists POC eligibility for certain former Pakistani citizens, descendants of Pakistani citizens and qualifying family relationships. Some spouse-based categories have specific restrictions; check the current rules before applying.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Establish your citizenship position

Work out whether you are currently a Pakistani citizen, a former citizen or a foreign national of Pakistani descent. If uncertain, seek confirmation rather than self-classifying.

02

Read NADRA’s eligibility pages

Compare the NICOP definition with the new POC eligibility rules, particularly where your claim depends on a parent, grandparent or spouse.

03

Collect the correct supporting link

For a POC, prepare relevant proof of origin or family connection. For NICOP, review the identity and citizenship requirements applicable to your case.

04

Apply through the official channel

Use NADRA PakID for the supported category. Keep your recorded spelling and family data consistent.

05

Confirm entry and residence implications

A POC has particular entry and administrative privileges, but it is not the same legal status as Pakistani citizenship. Confirm any destination-specific travel rules separately.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • POC is not proof of Pakistani citizenship.
  • Acquiring another nationality does not by itself tell you whether you have retained Pakistani citizenship.
  • Do not rely on social-media advice about spouse eligibility; restrictions appear in NADRA’s policy.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreign-born child get NICOP?

Possibly, if the child is a Pakistani citizen and satisfies NADRA’s requirements. Establish citizenship and registration first.

Does POC replace a Pakistani passport?

No. It is an identity/status document for eligible foreign nationals, not a Pakistani passport.

Can a former Pakistani citizen apply for POC?

NADRA explicitly lists certain former Pakistani citizens among eligible applicants; check the required evidence.
