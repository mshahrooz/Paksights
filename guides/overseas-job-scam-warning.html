Avoid overseas recruitment scams: what to verify before paying

Protect yourself and family members from fake foreign job offers and impersonated agencies.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

For employment abroad from Pakistan, check whether the recruiter is appropriately authorized through the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment (BEOE) and confirm the employer and offer independently.

What you should know first

Job scams often imitate real employers, offer unrealistic pay, and request fees urgently before a contract or visa can be verified. A genuine-looking letterhead or WhatsApp profile is not official confirmation.

The recruitment route, required protection process and restrictions differ by country and role. Use BEOE’s current information and the destination country’s own official visa guidance.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Verify the actual recruiter

Begin at beoe.gov.pk and locate the current official information for licensed overseas employment promoters. Check the licence identity and active status—not just a business card.

02

Validate the employer separately

Use an independently found company website or official switchboard rather than a phone number in the offer message.

03

Check visa and job conditions

Compare the offer against destination-country government rules, including visa type, salary conditions and any worker protections.

04

Ask for a written breakdown

Get the contract, duties, workplace, costs, refund rules and required process in writing before paying anything.

05

Never share account access

Do not provide banking OTPs, passwords or remote-control access to someone offering a job.

06

Record and report suspicious approaches

Keep transaction and message evidence. Contact the relevant regulator, law enforcement or mission if fraud is suspected.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • Being listed on a website is not necessarily proof of a currently valid licence; check active status.
  • A job or visa cannot be guaranteed merely by paying a private agent.
  • This guide does not provide country-specific immigration eligibility advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is any advance payment safe?

Verify the specific official charge and who is permitted to collect it; never send money on a promise alone.

Where should I confirm a Pakistani recruiter?

Start with the official Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment website.

What if I already paid a scammer?

Preserve evidence, contact your payment provider immediately and report to relevant enforcement authorities.
