Property occupied or disputed in Pakistan? How to organize your complaint

A sensible evidence-first approach, including help from the Overseas Pakistanis Foundation.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

The Overseas Pakistanis Foundation (OPF) accepts complaints from eligible overseas Pakistanis, including property disputes and illegal occupation, and coordinates with relevant authorities. OPF does not replace courts or act as your personal litigation counsel.

What you should know first

Many property complaints become harder to resolve because documents, notices and old case numbers are scattered between relatives and offices. A one-page timeline can help the authority see the issue clearly.

OPF says it may request overseas status and supporting records and can coordinate with agencies or authorities, but it does not directly provide court representation.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Collect primary proof

Organize registered deeds, ownership records, payment proofs, possession records, legal notices and past complaints in date order.

02

Write a factual timeline

State when you acquired the property, when the alleged interference started, who is involved and what remedy you seek.

03

Identify legal urgency

If a filing deadline, threatened eviction or immediate safety issue exists, consult a Pakistan-qualified lawyer promptly rather than awaiting a complaint referral.

04

Use OPF’s complaint route

Consult the OPF complaint cell page and follow its Online Complaint Management System directions.

05

Include overseas status and focal contact

OPF lists identity, overseas documents, complaint evidence and a focal person in Pakistan among its requirements.

06

Keep all reference numbers

Track the OPF case and separate it from police, revenue or court proceedings. Send follow-up evidence when asked.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • OPF complaint handling is free according to its February 2026 public notice.
  • OPF cannot promise a legal outcome or replace an attorney.
  • Never pay an individual claiming to speed up an OPF complaint.

Frequently asked questions

Does OPF handle illegal occupation cases?

OPF lists property disputes and illegal occupation among the types of complaints it facilitates.

Will OPF represent me in court?

OPF says it does not offer direct legal representation.

Does filing cost money?

OPF’s public notice says its online complaint and processing services are free.
