IBCC equivalence for children who studied abroad

How to prepare overseas school qualifications for admission in Pakistan.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) assesses equivalence of qualifying foreign and local secondary/higher-secondary qualifications with Pakistan’s SSC and HSSC levels. Applicants can start through its official online portal.

What you should know first

Families returning from the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Gulf states and elsewhere may have very different transcripts. IBCC evaluates subjects and awarding bodies, so not every school transcript automatically receives the requested equivalence.

IBCC provides an application process with online, appointment and courier options; the document list can vary by education system. Checking the exact qualification rules before leaving the old school can prevent delays.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Confirm the receiving institution’s need

Ask the Pakistani school, college or university which SSC/HSSC equivalence and subject group are required.

02

Check the awarding body

Review IBCC’s recognized qualifications and country-specific requirements.

03

Collect complete education history

Keep official Grades 9–12 records as applicable, transcripts, certificates and subject descriptions; request verification from the issuing body early.

04

Prepare translations and attestations

If records are not in English or Urdu, check IBCC’s translation policy. Some foreign qualifications require specific verification or attestations.

05

Apply through IBCC’s portal

Choose the available online, appointment or courier submission method based on your document requirements.

06

Track questions and deficiencies

Respond to IBCC requests for missing subjects, verification or corrections before arranging admission deadlines.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • IBCC handles qualifications generally up to HSSC; foreign higher-education degrees are a different process.
  • Do not assume online application means no original or verified records will ever be required.
  • Requirements differ for O/A Levels, American and other systems.

Frequently asked questions

Is equivalence available for American or Canadian school credentials?

IBCC states it handles recognized foreign secondary qualifications; exact criteria depend on the awarding system.

Can I apply before moving to Pakistan?

IBCC offers online submission routes; the required document verification and any in-person component depend on the case.

Is a bachelor’s degree handled by IBCC?

No. Higher education degree equivalence generally falls under HEC instead.
