Top-rated desi restaurants in Islamabad: 7 well-reviewed places (2026)

Looking for a proper karahi, a comforting plate of haleem or a memorable family BBQ dinner? These seven Islamabad restaurants have substantial public review histories and distinct strengths. Compare the food, setting and possible drawbacks before deciding where to go.

PakSights editorial desk · Published and checked 11 November 2026 · No sponsored placements

Where should you eat?

For buttery karahi: consider Butt Karahi in I-8 or F-10. For Shinwari-style shared platters: Zaviya is worth researching. For a quieter, homestyle meal: look at Dil Kay Desi. For family-style mixed desi dining: Tandoori in Faisal Town. For central F-6: Aanch. For traditional nashta: Chattha's.

These are editorial recommendations based on reported ratings, volume and review trends—not personal restaurant visits or a definitive ranking of every Islamabad restaurant.

How we selected these restaurants

We reviewed publicly accessible restaurant listings and reporting about Google ratings, noting both star scores and review volumes. A high score from a handful of customers was not treated as stronger evidence than a similar score supported by thousands of reviews. We also looked for variety: karahi, tandoori food, Shinwari-style BBQ, homestyle dishes and traditional breakfast.

This is not a live Google top-seven leaderboard. Rating data comes from public listings and directories which may update at different times, including Restaurant Guru, Mulk, SuchScore and Google search/listing results. Where older sources disagree, the newest available observed snapshot is shown but may already have changed. We have not personally visited or ordered from these restaurants.

Google Maps images and quoted customer reviews have deliberately not been copied. For them, click the direct listing links, where Google maintains the original context, authorship and current information.

Check before visiting

Phone the restaurant to verify the correct branch, current opening hours, menu availability, service fees, payment options and family seating. Prices and buffet offerings may change. If you are an overseas visitor arranging a wider family outing, consider transport time and accessibility as well as food preference.
