How to verify Punjab land records before buying from overseas

Use the Punjab Land Records Authority as a starting point, then verify the actual title.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) provides land-record services, including verification and access to Fard and computerized land information. Online lookup is useful, but a transaction still requires careful legal verification.

What you should know first

A screenshot of land ownership, seller-provided photocopy or marketing brochure alone is not enough to establish marketable title. Public land records, sale deeds, mutation records, approvals and physical possession can tell different parts of the story.

PLRA says overseas Pakistanis can access certain land services remotely through digital platforms and mission-related facilitation. Exact coverage can differ by locality and property type.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Get exact property identifiers

Collect district, tehsil, mouza, khasra/khewat details or the relevant plot information from the seller before searching.

02

Visit the official PLRA site

Start at punjab-zameen.gov.pk and find the available public verification or service route. Avoid third-party lookalike domains.

03

Obtain current verified records

Request a current Fard or certified relevant record where the service permits it, and confirm the seller’s identity against the official record.

04

Cross-check the title chain

Have a locally qualified property lawyer examine registration documents, mutations, prior transfers and any encumbrance or litigation risk.

05

Confirm the issuing authority

Housing societies, development authorities and revenue records can have different roles. Check layout approval, dues and transfer requirements directly.

06

Only then plan payment

Prefer documented bank transfers, written agreements and independent professional oversight over informal cash or a relative’s assurance.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • A digital record search is not a guarantee that a property has no dispute.
  • Not every urban plot or society property is covered identically by rural revenue records.
  • Never treat advance payment as a substitute for legal due diligence.

Frequently asked questions

Can I verify ownership from abroad?

PLRA describes remote facilities for overseas Pakistanis; available services depend on the record and transaction.

Is a Fard enough to buy safely?

No. Legal title, registered deeds, encumbrances and society or authority status also require checking.

Should I authorize a relative to handle the deal?

Only with carefully limited authority, transparent records and independent review.
