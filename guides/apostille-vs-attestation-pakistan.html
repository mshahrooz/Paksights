Apostille or general attestation? Pakistan’s updated document process

A guide to choosing the right service under MOFA’s newer 2026 digital system.

PakSights research deskPublished 10 Nov 2026 · Sources reviewed 10 Nov 2026
In brief

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has digital attestation services for Apostille and General Attestation. The correct choice depends on the destination country, whether the Apostille Convention applies, and what the receiving institution specifically accepts.

What you should know first

MOFA announced a new digital attestation system effective October 12, 2026 for Islamabad and listed liaison offices. Other MOFA pages describe online registration, review and appointment rules; follow the currently active portal instructions for your document and service.

An Apostille authenticates the origin of an eligible public document. It does not certify that every statement in the document is true or guarantee acceptance by a foreign university, employer or immigration authority.

Step-by-step: what to do

Work through these actions in order. The official service may ask for additional information depending on your circumstances.

01

Ask the recipient first

Get the receiving institution’s exact requested form: Apostille, general attestation, translation or additional legalization.

02

Check the country directory

Use MOFA’s current attestation portal and destination guidance to see whether Apostille applies.

03

Confirm your document is eligible

Birth, degree, police and commercial records may require prior authentication by the issuing authority before MOFA can process them.

04

Create the official digital application

Use MOFA’s official attestation portal to enter details and upload required documents.

05

Follow the live submission route

Comply with the current digital certificate, appointment or collection instructions shown for your category; procedures have changed during 2026.

06

Keep verification evidence

Save the issued certificate, reference and the receiving office’s acceptance requirements.

Common mistakes to avoid

Look out for these
  • Do not follow an old article claiming walk-in attestation is always available.
  • Apostille is not accepted for every destination or every document.
  • Published 2026 procedures have changed; check the official portal when applying.

Frequently asked questions

What changed in October 2026?

MOFA announced a digital attestation system effective October 12, 2026. Follow its current portal rather than older counter-only instructions.

Do all countries accept Apostille?

No. MOFA provides destination guidance; acceptance also depends on the receiving authority and applicable treaty relationships.

Does Apostille replace a certified translation?

Not necessarily. The receiving institution may still require a translation.
